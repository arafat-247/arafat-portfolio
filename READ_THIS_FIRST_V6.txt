- separate topic browsing
- pinned stories support
- In Focus homepage module
- this-week / this-month output counts
- About page
- CV / Resume page with Print / Save PDF
- Contact page
- Featured Investigations / Depth Reporting page
- awards and publications sections
- improved weighted search
- related stories

- automatic quote card where a real quote is available
- optional curated charts, timelines, maps, quotes and explainers
- local story footer and related-story modules

VISUAL SAFEGUARD
Charts, maps and timelines are not invented. Put manually verified modules in:
site/data/story_visuals.json

PIN STORIES
Put Daily Star source URLs in site/data/site_config.json under pinned_story_urls.

FEATURED INVESTIGATIONS
Put verified source URLs under featured_investigation_urls.

CONTACT EMAIL
Add your preferred public email under contact_email.
