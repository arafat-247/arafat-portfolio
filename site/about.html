I’m Arafat Rahaman, a journalist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I report on education, governance, human rights, social welfare and public institutions.

Dhaka, Bangladesharafat.mcj@yahoo.com

My journey

My journalism began on campus, reporting on student issues before moving into full-time newsroom work. I now work at The Daily Star, covering public institutions and the policies and decisions that affect everyday life.

My approach is grounded in verification, clear writing and accountable reporting, with particular attention to education, governance, social welfare and human rights.
