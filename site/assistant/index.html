FIRST USE

Three steps to a first task

  1. 01

    Download and extract

    Extract the whole ZIP. Keep ArafatAssistant.exe and its _internal folder together, then open the app.

  2. 02

    Prepare a local model

    Open Start Local Assistant.cmd, then Local setup. Install Ollama using the official installer and download a Light or Standard model. No OpenAI sign-in is used in Local mode.

  3. 03

    Give it one task

    Record or type your instruction. Check the transcript and review proposed website or app actions before allowing them.

What to expect

You review the actions

Voice is recorded after you press the button. Transcripts can be edited before execution. Stop takes effect between operations.

The work runs locally

Your Windows companion handles the browser and supported app controls. A public portfolio page cannot operate your computer on its own.

A model on your PC

Local mode processes task context on your PC with Ollama cloud features disabled. Model downloads and website tasks need internet. Optional ChatGPT/API modes send context to OpenAI and have their own eligibility and billing.

Test on your computer

Model speed depends on your hardware. Small local models can make mistakes. Start with one short task. Voice needs its own speech-model download. This is an unsigned preview; keep Windows security protections enabled.
