ARAFAT RAHAMAN PORTFOLIO — V10

V10 is a full front-end reset while retaining the verified Daily Star scraper and GitHub Pages deployment pipeline.

VISIBLE SITE
- Home: new portrait-led personal portfolio layout.
- Work: searchable chronological archive with Reporting / Opinion & Analysis filters.
- Photography: curated-gallery framework plus Flickr archive link.
- About: professional profile, experience, coverage, recognition and factual portfolio numbers.
- Contact: professional contact, profile links and Send a News Tip section.

STORY PAGES
- No forced generic hero image.
- A story image appears only when it has been deliberately configured in site/data/story_enhancements.json.
- Small Arafat Rahaman headshot appears with the full original byline.
- Complete co-bylines are preserved.
- Published date and reading time are visible.
- Slim share/print rail on desktop; compact share bar on mobile.
- Pull quotes, charts, timelines, tables and other modules are inserted inside the article only when curated.
- Real tables become searchable/sortable and can be downloaded as CSV.
- Original Daily Star attribution appears at the end.
- Related coverage can appear at the end.
- Newer/older story navigation has been removed.

IMPORTANT UPLOAD RULES
1. Upload/replace everything in the V10 site-only package inside your GitHub site/ folder.
2. Upload/replace all backend files at their exact paths:
   .github/workflows/deploy.yml
   scripts/update_articles.py
   scripts/build_static.py
   scripts/optimise_images.py
   scripts/check_budget.py
   requirements.txt
3. DO NOT manually upload or create:
   site/data/articles.json
   site/data/archive-index.json
   site/data/story_art_queue.json
   The workflow generates these.
4. V10 uses ?v=10 on CSS/JS links to reduce stale-browser-cache problems during deployment.

STORY IMAGES
V10 deliberately does not invent a generic image for every article. Add a curated story image by placing optimised files in site/assets/story-covers/ and adding the exact Daily Star URL to site/data/story_enhancements.json.

PHOTOGRAPHY
The gallery is ready but is not filled with invented images. Add curated photographs to site/assets/photography/ and entries to site/data/photography.json.
